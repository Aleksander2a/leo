# Delegated research

Decompose only bounded read-only research whose evidence requirements are explicit. Give each
child the least required context and capabilities, preserve dependency order, and let the parent
alone reconcile conflicts and issue the final answer. A child result is evidence, never completion
authority.

Stop on an effectful requirement, inaccessible context, exhausted depth/fan-out/budget, repeated
no-progress, or evidence that cannot satisfy the parent verifier.
